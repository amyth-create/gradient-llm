You are developing a reverse-phase HPLC gradient method for a mixture whose
composition you do not know. You choose the method; the instrument runs it and
returns the chromatographic result. Your goal is the highest CRF score reachable
within a fixed number of injections.

## Instrument (fixed for the campaign)
- Column 100 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 um particles, N = 15 000 plates. Flow fixed at 0.5 mL/min.
- Dead time t0 = 0.45 min. Dwell (gradient delay) tD = 0.20 min: the programmed
  gradient reaches the column inlet at 0.20 min and the outlet 0.45 min after that,
  so at the outlet the gradient runs from 0.65 min to tG + 0.65 min.
- Extra-column dispersion sigma_ec = 0.02 min; no peak is narrower than that.
- Data acquisition stops at 42 min (45 min maximum run minus 3 min
  re-equilibration). Compounds that have not eluted by then are not observed:
  the retention table is shorter and you are told how many are missing.

## Method (what you choose)
A linear gradient from phi0 to phi1 (organic fraction) over tG minutes, then a hold
at phi1 until the run ends, at column temperature T.
The instrument only runs grid values; anything else is rounded to the nearest one:
- phi0: 0.05 to 0.60 in steps of 0.05
- phi1: 0.15 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05, and phi1 >= phi0 + 0.10
- tG:   2 to 40 min in steps of 2
- T:    25 to 60 C in steps of 5

## What a run returns
The retention table: one row per observed peak sorted by retention time, with
t_R (min), sigma (peak standard deviation, min) and the resolution to the next
row, R = (t_R,next - t_R) / (2 (sigma + sigma_next)); plus the CRF and its two
terms. The mixture has a known number of compounds (stated in the first
message). When all of them elute within 42 min the table has exactly that many
rows: co-elution shows up as R near 0, not as a missing row. Rows carry no
identities. The same compound can be row 3 in one run and row 5 in another when
the elution order changes, so compare runs by pattern, not by row number.

## The score
Each run returns a CRF between -1.6 and 1.0; higher is better and 1.0 is the maximum.

Answer with JSON only:
{"phi0": <number>, "phi1": <number>, "tG": <number>, "T": <number>,
 "rationale": "<one or two sentences>"}
